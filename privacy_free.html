Privacy Policy — Raina Free

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Raina Free ("the App"), developed by desckop04, handles your information. Raina Free is the free, offline edition of Raina.

Everything Stays on Your Device

Raina Free runs entirely on your device. Everything you type is processed locally using a built-in offline model. Your messages are never sent to any server. There is no cloud AI, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

All your data — profile, chat history, notes, game progress, and app settings — is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Microphone

The App may request access to your device's microphone solely to enable voice recognition. Audio is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition and is not stored or shared by us.

Feedback

The App includes an optional feedback button. If you choose to send feedback, only the message text you write is transmitted to the developer to help improve the App. This is entirely voluntary and is never sent automatically. We do not attach your name or any device identifier to it.

Third-Party Content

The drawing feature uses doodles from Google's "Quick, Draw!" dataset, licensed under CC BY 4.0. This content contains no personal data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review it periodically.
