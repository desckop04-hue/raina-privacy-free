Privacy Policy — Raina Free

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Raina Free ("the App"), developed by desckop04, handles your information. Raina Free is the free, offline edition of Raina.

Everything Stays on Your Device

Raina Free runs entirely on your device. Everything you type to Raina is processed locally using a built-in offline model. Your conversations are never sent to any server. There is no cloud AI, no advertising, and no background analytics or tracking. The only information that ever leaves your device is the optional feedback you choose to send (see Feedback below).

All your data — profile, chat history, notes, game progress, and app settings — is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Microphone

The App may request access to your device's microphone solely to enable voice recognition, and only at the moment you tap the voice button — never in the background. Audio is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition and is not stored or shared by us.

Feedback

The App includes an optional feedback button, and may also show a prompt inviting you to rate the App. Sending feedback is entirely voluntary and is never sent automatically. If you choose to send it, the following is transmitted to the developer to help improve the App: the message text you write, your star rating, the App's version and language, and two anonymous usage figures — how many times you have opened the App and how many days have passed since you installed it. These figures only help us understand whether people keep using the App. We do not attach your name or any device identifier, and you cannot be personally identified from this information.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review it periodically.
