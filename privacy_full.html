Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Raina ("the App"), developed by desckop04, handles your information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

The App requests access to your device's microphone solely to enable voice recognition features. Audio is processed by your device's built-in speech recognition and is not stored or shared by us.

Artificial Intelligence

Raina uses the Groq API (groq.com) to generate responses. When you send a message to Raina, that text is transmitted to Groq's servers for processing. Groq uses Meta's Llama language model to generate responses. We do not store your conversations on our servers. Please refer to Groq's Privacy Policy for details: https://groq.com/privacy-policy

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and use data from your device (such as advertising identifiers) to serve ads. This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Feedback

The App includes an optional feedback button. If you choose to send feedback, only the message text you write is transmitted to the developer to help improve the App. This is entirely voluntary and is never sent automatically. We do not attach your name or any device identifier to it.

Third-Party Content

The drawing feature uses doodles from Google's "Quick, Draw!" dataset, licensed under CC BY 4.0. This content contains no personal data.

Data Storage

All local data (notes, chat history, game progress, app settings) is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review it periodically.
